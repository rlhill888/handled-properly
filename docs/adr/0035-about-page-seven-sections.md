# /about grows into seven named sections, each with its own image, replacing the five-piece version from 0034

[`0034`](./0034-about-page-editable-fields-not-blocks.md) made `/about`'s existing five pieces (hero, story, differentiators, testimonial, CTA) admin-editable. The admin asked for a specific, larger structure instead: Hero/Introduction, Our Story, Who We Are, What We Do, Our Mission, Our Vision, and Our Values, each with its own admin-editable text *and exactly one admin-editable image*, plus the closing CTA stays (every other marketing page on this site ends on one; dropping the page's only action wasn't part of the ask).

**Testimonial is dropped.** It wasn't one of the seven requested sections, and — per [`0031`](./0031-about-page-hand-coded-not-block-driven.md)'s own reasoning — it was only ever a clearly-marked placeholder anyway, no real quote was ever entered. `site_about_page` drops `testimonial_quote`/`testimonial_attribution` rather than carrying two unused columns forward.

**"Differentiator" becomes "Value."** The three-card grid ADR-0034 built (title + short description, admin-managed count) is structurally identical to what "Our Values" (3–6 principles, each with a short explanation) needs — rather than keep a redundant "what makes us different" grid alongside a new, separately-built Values section, `site_about_differentiators` is renamed to `site_about_values` in place (same shape, same admin add/edit/remove/reorder UI, just relabeled `AboutValue`/`ValueList`/etc. throughout) and its seed rows were reworded from competitive claims to value statements.

**Each of the seven sections gets its own distinct visual treatment**, reusing techniques already established elsewhere on the site rather than inventing new ones per section: Hero keeps a load-time staggered fade-up (same technique as the homepage Hero.tsx, since it's already in view on page load with nothing to scroll past); Our Story and Who We Are are side-by-side image+text, text/image order flipped between them; What We Do is full-bleed with this site's drifting wave/grid line-art motif (same technique as the homepage's Hero/BuiltForMoments/ClosingCta); Our Mission is a single photo "statement" card with a gradient scrim and large centered text (the same full-bleed-photo + scrim + overlaid-text technique the `/about-and-connect` profile card uses), inset within its own section rather than taking the section over; Our Vision reuses that same profile card's one-shot shine sweep on its own image as its distinguishing flourish; Our Values keeps its existing card-grid shape with one new banner photo above it; the closing CTA matches the homepage's own `ClosingCta` (dark, same line-art motif) to bookend the page.

**Sections alternate white/black all the way down**, not just What We Do and the CTA reading dark against otherwise-white neighbors: Hero white, Our Story black, Who We Are white, What We Do black, Our Mission white (with its photo statement card as a dark inset *within* the white section, not the section itself going dark), Our Vision black, Our Values white, CTA black. Each section declares its own background explicitly rather than relying on inheriting the page's white default, so the rhythm holds regardless of what's above or below. Our Story and Our Vision both reuse the same side-by-side text markup Who We Are uses (`.storyText`/`.storyParagraph`/`.kicker`); rather than fork that shape into light- and dark-text copies, they get a scoped `.story .storyParagraph`/`.vision .storyParagraph` (and `.kicker`) override for light text, since only those two of the three need it.

**Black sections are full-bleed on desktop, white ones aren't.** `.page` lays every section out in a 3-column grid (`1fr minmax(0, 1440px) 1fr`) so its content centers at 1440px; by default every section sits in the middle column, leaving the outer two columns (the gutters past 1440px on a wide screen) unpainted. That's invisible for a white section — the gutter is already the same white as the page background — but it left visible white bars down both edges of every *black* section on wide viewports, when the ask was for the black to reach the true edges of the screen. Fix: `.story`, `.what`, and `.vision` (the three full-width black sections; Our Mission/Our Values/the CTA were already inset cards, not edge-to-edge, so this doesn't apply to them) span `grid-column: 1 / -1` — the whole grid, gutters included — while their actual content re-centers at 1440px via an inner wrapper (`.sectionRow` for Our Story/Who We Are/Our Vision's shared side-by-side shape, `.whatContent` for What We Do, which already had its own inner wrapper for the line-art SVG's sake). Who We Are picks up the same `grid-column: 1 / -1` as its two black neighbors even though it doesn't strictly need it, since it now shares the identical `.sectionRow` structure — simpler than special-casing the one white section built the same way as two black ones.

**Scroll animation, not just a one-shot reveal.** Sections 2 through 8 use `ScrollReveal` (the same continuous, scroll-position-driven fade/rise used throughout the homepage — `BuiltForMoments`, `TrustedBy`, `Services`, `FeaturedEvents`, `ClosingCta`) instead of the simpler one-shot `Reveal` component `/about` used before, so scrolling through this page now has the same continuous "chase" feel scrolling through the homepage does, rather than a plainer fade-in-once-and-stop.

**Missing images degrade gracefully, not silently.** Hero and Our Story keep their original bundled placeholder fallback images (`/about/hero.png`, `/about/story.png`) when unset, matching 0034. Our Mission and Our Vision fall back to a plain dark gradient placeholder box (reusing the same box for both) so the scrim/shine treatment still has something to sit on; Who We Are, What We Do, and Our Values simply render without an image when none is set, since those sections were designed to hold up as text-only layouts.
